# Screenshot Checklist for Final Submission

The app zip includes a working local prototype, but the final BUS5001 submission should still include your own screenshots.

## Local app screenshots

Capture these from the app:

1. Home screen showing Q1 chatbot and Q3 triage panel.
2. Completed chatbot sample flow after clicking **Run sample flow**.
3. Captured Parameters panel showing intent, issue category, urgency and route.
4. Q3 JSON output after clicking **Use sample**.

## Dialogflow CX screenshots required by the brief

1. Dialogflow CX agent overview.
2. Flow map/pages.
3. Intents and training phrases.
4. Entity or parameter setup.
5. Route/conditional branch.
6. Fallback response.
7. Test conversation result.

## Video link to add

Record less than 2 minutes and upload to Microsoft Stream or Echo365. Add the accessible link in the final report.
