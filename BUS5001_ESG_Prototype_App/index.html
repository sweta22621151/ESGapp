Conversation Design

Flow Summary

1WelcomeExplains purpose and privacy boundary.
2Identify intentClassifies ESG incident, policy, status, or escalation.
3Capture parametersExtracts location, issue category, urgency and sensitivity.
4Branch routeChooses facilities, sustainability, procurement or inclusion pathway.
5FallbackRequests clarification if the message is unclear.
6EndpointCreates local ticket confirmation or recommends human escalation.

This prototype is for academic demonstration only. It does not connect to Dialogflow CX, cloud ticketing systems, Microsoft Stream, GitHub, or live organisational data.
